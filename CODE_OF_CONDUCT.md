# Code of Conduct

Hoard is built by people with different backgrounds and experience levels. We want taking part to be a good experience for everyone.

## Expected

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent; ask before assuming.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing other people's private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that break these rules, and may block people from the project temporarily or permanently. Report problems privately to the maintainers through the contact listed on the project's GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.
